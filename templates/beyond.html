Beyond the Board

What do truth, unity, charity, and technology look like in everyday life?

The questions explored in this exhibit do not stay on the bulletin board. They continue in classrooms, communities, careers, and daily choices.

About CSA-Biñan

  • Augustinian Education CSA-Biñan is a private Catholic, coeducational institution administered by the Augustinians.
  • Virtus et Scientia Its motto, “Virtue and Science,” joins intellectual growth with moral formation.
  • Veritas, Unitas, Caritas Truth, unity, and charity shape the school's culture and mission.
  • Tradition and Innovation Augustinian tradition inspires while innovation empowers future-ready learning.
  • Since 1985 For decades, CSA-Biñan has served students through Catholic and Augustinian education.

Computer Science and the Future

Technology is built by people. Behind every application, website, and intelligent system are individuals solving problems and creating tools for others.

  • Software Development Creating programs and applications used every day.
  • Cybersecurity Protecting systems, networks, and information.
  • Artificial Intelligence Designing systems that assist human decision-making.
  • Data Science Finding meaning in information and patterns.
  • Cloud and Network Systems Keeping modern digital services connected and available.

The challenge. Technical skill matters. Character matters too. The future needs people who can build powerful technology and use it wisely.

Caritas, Unitas, Veritas

  1. Caritas. Love expressed through service, compassion, and concern for others.
  2. Unitas. Building community through cooperation, respect, and shared purpose.
  3. Veritas. Seeking truth through learning, reflection, honesty, and wisdom.

In the age of AI. These values remain essential. Technology may change, but truth, unity, and charity continue to guide how people should learn, work, and serve.

Truth to seek. Unity to build. Charity to live.
