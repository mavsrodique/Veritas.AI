St. Thomas of Villanova

Augustinian friar, archbishop, and Father of the Poor.

A life in five dates

  1. 1486 Born in Fuenllana, Spain. He grew up in nearby Villanueva de los Infantes, from which he later took the name "of Villanova."
  2. 1516 After studying and teaching at the University of Alcalá, he entered the Augustinian Order at Salamanca and became known as a gifted preacher, teacher, and leader.
  3. 1544 Appointed Archbishop of Valencia by Emperor Charles V. He accepted reluctantly and dedicated himself to reform, education, and care for the poor.
  4. 1555 Died in Valencia on September 8 after a life marked by charity, humility, and service.
  5. 1658 Canonized by Pope Alexander VII. His example continues to inspire Augustinian communities, schools, and ministries throughout the world.
