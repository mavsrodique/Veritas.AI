Room V

Human Reflection

Why a board about technology is full of feathers.

A machine can process information. Only a person can reflect, choose, and grow.

What the feathers stand for

  • ReflectionPausing to look back at what happened and what it meant.
  • TransformationLetting what you notice change who you are becoming.
  • GrowthSlow, honest improvement, one habit and one choice at a time.
  • Self-discoveryFinding out who you are, including what you would rather not see.

The root. All four grow from Augustinian Interiority: return to yourself, examine your life honestly, and there seek the truth.

At the board

The questions behind the feathers live only on the bulletin board. When you are standing in front of it:

  1. Find the feathers on the board.
  2. Choose one and pull it gently.
  3. pull on the right for self reflection and pull on the left for veritas's response.
  4. Read what is written, slowly.
  5. Answer it silently and honestly. Nobody is checking.

One quiet minute

Before returning to the board, take sixty seconds of silence. Notice your thoughts, your concerns, and what the questions have awakened in you.

Ready when you are.

Not alone

Reflection begins within the person, but it does not end there. Augustine encouraged people to live as "one mind and one heart" on the way to God. What we discover about ourselves can deepen our relationships, strengthen communities, and inspire service to others.

Reflection is human work. Technology can assist us, but the work of conscience, wisdom, and love remains our own.
