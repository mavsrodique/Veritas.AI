REFLECTION QUESTIONS ABOUT AI, TRUTH, AND HUMAN DIGNITY

These questions do not have single correct answers. Their purpose is to encourage reflection, discussion, and responsible use of technology.

These questions are designed to encourage thoughtful reflection on Artificial Intelligence, technology, human dignity, truth, responsibility, and service. They do not seek simple answers but deeper understanding.

QUESTION:
What are the limitations of AI when it comes to determining the truth?

GUIDING REFLECTION:
AI can analyze information, recognize patterns, and generate responses, but it does not possess personal understanding, conscience, wisdom, or moral judgment. AI may produce inaccurate information, reflect bias, or present uncertainty with confidence. Human beings remain responsible for evaluating evidence and seeking truth.

KEY IDEAS:
Veritas
Critical Thinking
Intellectual Humility
Wisdom
Responsibility

------------------------------------------------------------

QUESTION:
How can AI help professionals serve others while preserving the human side of their work?

GUIDING REFLECTION:
AI can assist teachers, doctors, researchers, counselors, and many other professionals by organizing information, reducing repetitive tasks, and improving access to knowledge. However, empathy, compassion, patience, understanding, and personal care remain uniquely human responsibilities. Technology should support service rather than replace relationships.

KEY IDEAS:
Caritas
Service
Human Dignity
Compassion
Responsibility

------------------------------------------------------------

QUESTION:
How can information provided by AI be verified for reliability?

GUIDING REFLECTION:
AI-generated information should be checked against trusted sources, qualified experts, academic research, official publications, and reliable evidence. Responsible users verify information before accepting or sharing it. Truth requires investigation, honesty, and careful judgment.

KEY IDEAS:
Veritas
Integrity
Critical Thinking
Responsibility
Wisdom

------------------------------------------------------------

QUESTION:
How can technology be designed to empower human capabilities rather than replace them?

GUIDING REFLECTION:
Technology should strengthen learning, creativity, communication, problem-solving, and human development. Good technology helps people become more capable rather than more dependent. Human beings should remain active participants rather than passive consumers of technology.

KEY IDEAS:
Human Flourishing
Stewardship
Innovation
Responsibility
Dignity

------------------------------------------------------------

QUESTION:
What makes technology meaningful beyond its ability to function?

GUIDING REFLECTION:
Technology becomes meaningful when it serves people, strengthens communities, respects dignity, promotes justice, and contributes to the common good. The value of technology is measured not only by efficiency or power but also by its impact on human lives.

KEY IDEAS:
Veritas
Caritas
Unitas
Human Dignity
Common Good
Service
